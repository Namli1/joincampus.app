Delete Your Account

We understand that you may want to delete your campus account. This page explains how you can request account deletion through two convenient methods.

⚠️ Important: Deleting your account is permanent. All your data, including your profile and down requests will be removed and cannot be recovered.

Method 1: Delete via the App Settings (Immediate)

The fastest way to delete your account is directly through the app:

  1. Open the campus app on your device
  2. Navigate to Settings
  3. Look for the Account section
  4. Select Delete Account
  5. Confirm your request when prompted
  6. Your account will be deleted immediately

Method 2: Request Deletion via Email

If you prefer to request account deletion by email, send a message to:

In your email, please include:

  • The email address used for your campus account
  • A clear statement that you request to delete your account

Our support team will process your deletion request and confirm once your account has been removed.

What Happens After Deletion?

  • Your account and all associated data will be permanently deleted
  • You will no longer be able to log in with your credentials
  • Your profile will no longer be visible to other users
  • You may re-register with the same email address after deletion, but this will create a new account with no previous data

Questions or Issues?

If you have any questions about the account deletion process or encounter any issues, please contact our support team at the email address shown above.
